library(remotes)
install_bitbucket("cloudfolks/tagbio", ref = "master", subdir = "tagbio")

Once installed, load the library.

Next, we connect to our local tagbio server. This should be running in a terminal on your computer or laptop and accepting requests at localhost:8000. When running locally no URL or authentication is required to make a connection.

tag_con <- tagConnect()

We next access the data product being served by the local server. We do not need to specify which data product we are accessing because only one is being served.

fc <- tbl(tag_con)

Let’s look at what data collections are available for this data product. Think of data collection as columns in a data frame.
